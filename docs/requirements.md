# Voxel Survival Game — Requirements (Source of Truth)

High-level source of truth for development agents. No technical details—product intent only.

**Goal:** First-person voxel survival game as a **direct Minecraft recreation** to establish a clear baseline. Use Minecraft’s design as the reference for systems, feel, and scope.

---

## Focus Areas

- **Procedural voxel generation** — World is built from blocks; terrain, caves, and structures are generated procedurally.
- **Multiple biomes** — Distinct regions (forest, desert, ocean, etc.) with different blocks, vegetation, and resources.
- **Breaking and editing** — Players break blocks (with tools) and place blocks from inventory to modify the world.
- **Day/night cycle** — Time advances; day vs night affects visibility and danger (e.g., hostile mobs at night).
- **Crafting** — Combine materials (from blocks and mob drops) into tools, weapons, armor, and blocks via recipes.
- **A* pathfinding** — Enemies and NPCs use A* to navigate the voxel world toward targets (e.g., player).
- **Health & death** — Player has health; damage from mobs, fall, etc. Death: respawn (e.g., at bed or world spawn), with possible item drop/loss.
- **Multiplayer** — Multiple players share the same world; play together, build together, survive together.

---

## Summary for Agents

**First-person Minecraft-like voxel game: procedural world, biomes, break/place blocks, day/night, crafting, A* for mobs/NPCs, health/death, multiplayer. Use Minecraft as the design baseline.**
